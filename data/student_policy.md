# Student Policy Handbook — SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT

> **This document is synthetic demonstration data written for a course project.
> It is NOT an official institutional policy. Do not rely on it for real decisions.**

## 1. Fee Payments

Programme fees are paid in three instalments during the academic year. Each instalment has a due date shown on the student's fee record. Fees can be paid through the online payment portal, by bank transfer, or by demand draft at the Accounts Office. A payment receipt is issued within 2 working days of the payment being received.

Students can check their fee status at any time through the Student Support Agent or the Accounts Office.

## 2. Payment Failures

If the online payment portal fails or a transaction does not complete, do not retry more than twice. Note the transaction reference number and take a screenshot of the error. If money has been debited but the payment is not reflected, it normally appears within 3 working days.

A payment made on the next working day after a documented portal failure is accepted without a late fee, provided the student reports the failure to the Accounts Office within 24 hours and shares the transaction reference or screenshot. Without documentation, the normal late fee applies.

## 3. Late Fees

A late fee of INR 500 per week (or part of a week) is charged on any instalment that remains unpaid after its due date. The late fee is capped at INR 5,000 per instalment. Late fees are not covered by scholarships.

## 4. Scholarships

The institute offers merit-based and need-based scholarships. Applications are reviewed within 15 working days of submission. Approved scholarship amounts are adjusted against the next fee instalment. A scholarship status can be: Not Applied, Under Review, Approved, or Rejected. Rejected applicants may re-apply in the next cycle.

## 5. Course Changes

Elective course changes are allowed within the first 2 weeks of a term. A course change is a request, not an automatic change: it takes effect only after the Academic Office approves it. Approval depends on seat availability in the requested course. Course changes are not possible after the 2-week window except in exceptional circumstances reviewed by the Academic Coordinator.

## 6. Support Requests

Students can raise a support request in one of these categories: Fees, Scholarship, Course Change, Technical, or Other. Every request receives a ticket number and starts with the status Open. The Student Support Desk responds within 2 working days. Urgent matters should be marked with High priority.

## 7. Escalation Procedures

If a support request is not resolved within 5 working days, the student may escalate it. Level 1 is the Student Support Desk. Level 2 is the Academic Coordinator. Level 3 is the Dean's Office. Escalate to the next level only after the previous level has had the chance to respond.

## 8. Privacy and Security

Students must never share passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), PINs, or card details with anyone, including support staff or this assistant. Students may only view their own fee and scholarship records. Staff will never ask for an OTP or password.
